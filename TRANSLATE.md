# Compatibility terminology history

`.translate` was the umbrella term used before semantic and lexical compatibility were separated.

- `.ttl` is the modern Tensor Translation Layer for semantic or internal model compatibility.
- `.ltl` is the modern Lexical Translation Layer for lexical or output compatibility.

Active users should read [TTL.md](TTL.md) and [LTL.md](LTL.md). Archived experiments and historical update records retain `.translate` in their original names so their provenance remains intact.
